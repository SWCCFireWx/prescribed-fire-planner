About this page

Purpose: a planning tool to help find the best upcoming prescribed burn windows anywhere in the U.S., up to 7 days out. It shows when ventilation (smoke dispersion), wind, humidity, temperature and precipitation line up with your burn prescription, and flags fire weather alerts, smoke-sensitive areas and overnight smoke settling.

For planning only, not specifics. Use it to narrow down which days and hours look promising, not for exact values or go/no-go decisions on burn day. The forecasts are automated model guidance (NOAA NBM and HRRR) with no forecaster review, and they can miss local effects such as valley inversions and drainage winds.

Before you burn: get a NWS spot forecast, follow your burn plan and smoke permit, and for tactical planning help contact your local Predictive Services forecaster.

How to use it:

  • Pick an area, then set the burn-window criteria to your prescription. Turn off any you don't need.
  • Use the Burn-window hours layer and the day buttons to spot good days.
  • Click the map for a point's 7-day forecast and its burn windows in local time.
